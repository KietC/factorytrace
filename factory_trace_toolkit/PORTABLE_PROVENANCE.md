# Portable provenance / 可迁移来源说明

## English

This source-portable toolkit contains reusable engineering methods:

- structured product, party, site, process and evidence records;
- contradiction review, source independence and evidence preservation;
- official documentation for reverse-image search, evidence preservation, company/plant verification, WaterMark certification, customs/origin records and platform access rules;
- deterministic Python/Pillow scripts and their tests.

Case-specific originals, credentials, cookies, private communications, machine paths and current environment reports are not included in the portable source package.

Model and external-service use must be declared and logged per new case. This repository contains no historical case findings or model audit records.

Important corrections carried forward:

- a seller, licence holder, exporter or assembler is not automatically the target-process factory;
- a missing target process or contradictory equipment record prevents a confirmed attribution;
- same-image and platform evidence discovers candidates but cannot confirm tooling or deep drawing;
- current conclusions must come from structured evidence and a fresh audit, never from an old report or manually typed probability.

Machine-specific source paths and full local case provenance are deliberately excluded from this repository.

## 中文

这个源码包保存可复用的方法，不保存原项目的业务档案：

- 产品、主体、地点、工艺与证据的结构化记录；
- 矛盾复核、来源独立性检查与证据保全；
- 反向图片搜索、企业与厂址核验、WaterMark、海关及原产地资料、平台访问规则的公开方法参考；
- 确定性的 Python/Pillow 脚本及测试。

原始案件材料、凭据、Cookie、私信、机器路径和现场环境报告不在包内。每个新案件必须重新声明并记录模型与外部服务使用情况；这里没有历史案件结论或模型审计记录。

保留的方法纠错规则：销售方、持证人、出口商和装配方不能自动等于目标工艺工厂；缺少目标工艺证据或设备记录矛盾时不能确认归属；同图和平台页面只用于发现候选，不证明模具或成形工艺；结论必须来自新证据和新审计，不能沿用旧报告或手填概率。
