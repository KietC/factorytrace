# Source Manufacturer and Certification Inquiry

## English

Subject: Verification of manufacturing site, target process, and certification scope for {{MODEL}}

Hello,

We are verifying manufacturing and certification information for `{{MODEL}}` ({{RATING_OR_DIMENSION}}). Please answer the following within a scope that does not disclose trade secrets:

1. What is your legal entity and the name/address of the actual manufacturing site for this model?
2. Are you the seller, licence holder, assembler, or complete manufacturer?
3. Is `{{TARGET_PROCESS}}` performed in-house at that site? If not, which legal entity and site perform it?
4. Who produces the principal body, functional mechanism, seals, and other critical components?
5. Who designs, makes, owns, and stores the tooling? Can an ID/revision be partially disclosed?
6. Can you provide non-confidential equipment plates, continuous production video, batch records, or audit confirmation?
7. Does certificate `{{CERTIFICATION}}` cover this exact model, ratings, and manufacturing site?
8. Have manufacturer, site, BOM, material, tooling, or design-lock changes occurred recently?
9. Can you provide batch CoC/test results/labels/CoO or redacted procurement/logistics evidence?

If a full address or subcontractor name is confidential, please at least confirm:

- Whether the target process occurs at the site audited by the certification body.
- Whether the licence holder is also the actual manufacturer.
- Whether the target component is a purchased critical component.

Please include the respondent's name, role, business email, response date, and an official source link or attachment that can be checked.

Thank you.

## 中文

# 源头厂家与认证询证模板

主题：核验 {{MODEL}} 的实际制造地点、目标工序与认证范围

您好，

我们正在核验产品 `{{MODEL}}`（{{RATING_OR_DIMENSION}}）的制造和认证信息。为避免询问商业机密，请在可披露范围内逐项确认：

1. 贵司法律主体和该型号的实际制造地点名称、地址是什么？
2. 贵司是销售方、Licence Holder、组装厂，还是完整制造商？
3. `{{TARGET_PROCESS}}` 是否在上述地点厂内完成？若不是，由哪一法律主体和地点完成？
4. 主体、功能机构、密封件和其他关键部件分别由谁生产？
5. 模具或工装由谁设计、开制、拥有和保管？模具编号/版本能否局部披露？
6. 能否提供不含商业机密的设备铭牌、连续生产视频、批次记录或审厂证明？
7. {{CERTIFICATION}} 证书是否覆盖此精确型号、额定值和制造地点？
8. 最近是否发生制造商、制造地点、BOM、材料、模具或 design-lock 变更？
9. 能否提供该批次的 CoC、测试结果、装箱标签、CoO 或经过遮盖的采购/物流证明？

如完整地址或外协厂名称受保密限制，请至少回答：

- 目标工序是否在认证机构审查的制造地点完成；
- 持证人是否同时为实际制造商；
- 目标部件是否属于外购 critical component。

请注明回复人姓名、职务、公司邮箱、回复日期及可供复核的官方链接或附件。

谢谢。
