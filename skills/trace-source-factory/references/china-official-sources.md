# Mainland China Official Sources

## English

### Entity resolution

Establish the full registered Chinese name, unified social credit code, former names, legal representative, shareholders, registered/operating addresses, incorporation date, and business scope. An English storefront name is not a verified legal identity.

Prioritize official corporate/market-regulation disclosures, trademark/patent registries, pollution permits, local environmental impact/acceptance/technical-upgrade notices, investment/energy/fire/electricity notices, courts/enforcement records, customs/procurement/tender notices, and quality-supervision records. Aggregators can reveal names/IDs; return to official records for decisive fields or state the access limitation.

### Reverse-search the required process

- Forging: heating furnaces, forging/trim presses, blasting, annealing, tonnage, gas, and emissions.
- Deep drawing: hydraulic drawing presses, blanking, drawing lubricant, trimming, and polishing.
- Die casting: furnaces, casting machines/alloys, emissions, and hazardous waste.
- Machining: CNC lathes/centres, cleaning, coolant, and metal chips.
- Plating: baths, wastewater, and hazardous chemicals; investigate outsourcing if corresponding capability is absent.
- Toolmaking: design, CNC, wire cutting/EDM, trials, and maintenance.

Environmental/permit records can distinguish forming from sales/assembly but establish declared or approved capability, not actual production of the target SKU.

### Timeline and supporting sources

For each document record project date, construction site, equipment count, capacity, process flow, product category, and current validity. A former/relocated site is not current-site evidence. Hiring ads reveal staffing needs, not commissioned equipment; patent ownership establishes an applicant/right, not in-house manufacturing.

### Site closure

Compare registered, environmental, hiring, map, certification, website, and dispatch addresses. A city/industrial park alone leaves the site unresolved. Shared directors or phones do not automatically merge companies. Use public records proportionately; collect only identity/business details needed for the manufacturing claim.

## 中文

# 中国内地官方源

## 主体解析

先固定企业中文全称、统一社会信用代码、曾用名、法定代表人、股东、注册地址、经营地址、成立时间和经营范围。不要把英文店铺名直接映射为法律主体。

优先检查：

- 国家企业信用信息公示系统及地方市场监管公示
- 中国商标网、国家知识产权局专利检索
- 全国排污许可证管理信息平台
- 地方生态环境局环评、验收、排污和技改公示
- 地方政府项目、工业投资、节能审查、消防和用电增容公示
- 中国裁判文书、执行信息和法院公告的主体/地址线索
- 海关、政府采购、招投标及质量监督公开信息

平台聚合页可帮助发现企业名和编号，但关键字段应回到官方记录或保存其限制。

## 设备与工序反查

围绕目标工序搜索官方或准官方文件：

- 热锻：铜棒加热炉、锻压机、切边机、抛丸、退火、吨位、燃气和排放。
- 深拉：液压拉伸机、压力机、板材开料、拉伸油、修边和抛光。
- 压铸：熔炉、压铸机、铝锌铜合金、废气和危废。
- 机加工：数控车床、加工中心、清洗、切削液和金属屑。
- 电镀：镀槽、废水、危化品；若无相应许可，考虑外协。
- 模具：模具设计、CNC、线切割、放电、试模和模具维修。

环评和排污文件常能区分销售/装配厂与真实成形厂，但文件只证明获批或申报能力，不必然证明目标 SKU 实际生产。

## 时间线

记录每份文件的项目年份、建设地点、设备数量、产能、工艺流程、产品类别和当前有效性。旧厂或已搬迁地址不得直接支持当前生产。

## 招聘与知识产权

招聘可显示岗位需求，例如油压拉伸工、锻压工、五金模具师傅、CNC 调机员和抛光工；专利可显示设计或申请主体。二者均为辅助证据：招聘不证明设备已投入，专利权也不证明自行生产。

## 地址闭环

交叉比较注册地址、环评地点、招聘地点、地图厂区、认证地点、官网地址和发货地址。地址只到园区或城市时保持地点未闭合；不要用同一法定代表人或同一电话自动合并不同公司。

适度使用公开记录，只收集制造归因所需的身份与业务信息。
