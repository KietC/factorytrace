# Social-Media and Marketplace Research

## English

### Search identities separately

Search legal/full/short/former names, domains, business emails/phones/contacts/addresses, SKU/certificate identifiers, and image filenames. Keep Chinese, English, romanization, former names, and brands as separate variants.

### International sources

Review LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, Alibaba, first-party/distributor sites, exhibitions, and public trade records. Look for earliest visible products/history; professional factory/procurement/tooling/engineering relationships; equipment plates/signage/workshop/tooling/process sequences in continuous video; upstream names in business documents; and cross-platform address/domain/contact reuse.

Company profiles/resumes may be self-reported, video may be reposted, and marketplaces usually establish a sales display. Record publisher and original date. Public follows, surnames, social connections, and personal accounts are leads, not proof of procurement role or manufacturing relationships; avoid unrelated private-life investigation.

### Domestic sources

Review authorized 1688/Taobao sessions, official sites, public business posts/video channels, marketplaces, and maps. Where authorized, submit logo-neutral whole/crop images; query dimensions/material/interfaces/ratings/process combinations; record store legal entity, registration history, factory profile/audit video, and first visible publication. Continue beyond resellers through partner-factory claims, labels, trade/manually provided business records, manuals, and workshop backgrounds.

Before any upload distinguish public product imagery from confidential drawings/private photos/customer documents. Ask for authorization before disclosing confidential material. Search and browser tools are environment-dependent; if unavailable record the limitation rather than claim completed searches.

### Contact graph, chronology, and friendly access

Use public business phones/emails/domains/addresses as nodes, with a source/date on each edge. Shared contacts may indicate a group, operator, or agent, not one factory. Compare CDN names, EXIF, dimensions, watermarks, crops/compression, and page dates. Earliest visible content is a clue, not necessarily the producer.

Use normal sessions and visible clicks; bound downloads, concurrency, and retries. Never bypass CAPTCHA/login/rate limits/access controls. Save receipts/screenshots for access failures and use `UNVERIFIED`. Deduplicate copied content by original source family. Research does not authorize messages, comments, likes, or downloads beyond the requested scope.

## 中文

# 国内外社媒与平台研究

## 搜索对象

对企业全称、简称、域名、邮箱、电话、联系人、地址、产品型号、证书号和图片文件名分别搜索。中文、英文、拼音、曾用名和品牌名分开执行。

## 国外平台

检查 LinkedIn、Facebook、YouTube、Instagram、Alibaba、企业官网、经销商官网、展会目录和公开进口记录。重点寻找：

- 最早产品发布时间和历史型号。
- 员工履历中的工厂、采购、模具和工程关系。
- 连续生产视频中的设备铭牌、厂牌、墙面、工装和工序顺序。
- 经销商或客户文件中的原制造商名称。
- 地址、电话、邮箱域名和联系人跨平台复用。

LinkedIn 公司页和个人履历可能由用户自填；Facebook/YouTube 视频可能转发；Alibaba 商品页通常只能证明销售展示。必须记录发布者和原始发布时间。

公开关注、姓氏、社交关联和个人账号只作线索，不证明采购职位或制造关系；不调查无关私生活。

## 国内平台

检查 1688、淘宝、企业官网、微信公众号、视频号、抖音、快手、B2B 店铺和地图。执行：

1. 上传去商标整图和局部结构图。
   上传前区分公开产品图与保密图纸/私密照片/客户文件；保密材料须先询问授权。
2. 搜尺寸、材质、接口、额定参数和工艺词组合。
3. 记录店铺公司主体、注册年份、工厂档案、验厂视频和商品首次发布时间。
4. 找到转售商后继续穿透合作工厂、包装、提单、说明书和视频背景。

## 联系图谱

把电话、邮箱、WhatsApp、微信、域名注册线索和地址建成节点。共享联系方式可能表示同集团、代运营或贸易代理，不自动表示同一工厂。给每条边标明来源和时间。

## 时间优先和内容指纹

比较图片 CDN 文件名、EXIF、像素尺寸、水印层、裁剪、压缩和页面发布时间。最早可见来源是重要线索，但不自动等于生产者；经销商可能比工厂更早公开。

## 友好访问

使用正常登录会话和可见交互，限制点击、下载、并发和重试。不要绕过验证码、登录、限速或访问控制。若平台策略阻止 DOM 读取，保存截图和阻断回执，状态写 `UNVERIFIED`。

工具能力因环境而异，不可用时如实记录，不声称已完成搜索。同源复制按来源族去重。研究请求不自动授权发送消息、评论、点赞或超范围下载。
