# Report Semantics and Publication Gates

## English

### Status vocabulary

- `CONFIRMED`: entity/site/exact-SKU/process closed by direct/strong evidence; critical contradictions resolved.
- `SUPPORTED`: consistent evidence exists, but at least one hard gate remains open.
- `INFERENCE`: reasoned from facts with the inference chain and alternatives stated.
- `LEAD`: worth investigating, not purchasing confirmation.
- `CONTRADICTED`: a key claim overturned by stronger evidence.
- `UNRESOLVED`: evidence insufficient or conflicting.
- `HOLD`: awaiting review after expiry, suspension, change, or new contradiction.

### Separate axes, not manufacturer probabilities

Report `trace_stage`, analytic confidence, ESS, capability fit, certification status, verification priority, and procurement utility separately. Never rename one axis “manufacturer probability” or invent uncalibrated percentages. If percentages are requested, explain that independent ground truth and external calibration are absent and provide the auditable axes instead. A high rank from pictures/family/trade does not identify the true factory while site/process remains unresolved.

### Facts, inference, and recommendations

Label directly supported facts, derivations with alternatives, and recommended inquiries/samples/site checks separately. Avoid certainty language inside fact statements when the source supports only a narrower observation.

### Contacts and summary

Where available, include business contact/phone/email/WeChat/WhatsApp/site/LinkedIn, source URL, and last verification time. Distinguish official publication, platform publication, historical material, and user verification. Do not guess unpublished numbers or collect unrelated private information.

The summary must state the leading candidate and supported scope, open gates, strongest contradiction/alternative, current certification status/date, and next highest-information action.

### Publication checklist

Check clickable URLs, real local paths, matching evidence hashes, ranking/body consistency, current versus expired certificates, blocked versus verified browser status, and absence of manufacturer percentages. An `operational PASS` is not factory confirmation. For public release, exclude private contacts, customer/supplier records, credentials, session data, and unredacted evidence; keep a separate protected case archive.

## 中文

# 报告语义与发布闸门

## 状态词

- `CONFIRMED`：主体、地点、精确 SKU 和目标工序均由直接/强证据闭合，关键反证关闭。
- `SUPPORTED`：有多条一致证据，但至少一个硬门槛尚未完全闭合。
- `INFERENCE`：由已知事实推导，清楚写出推导链和替代解释。
- `LEAD`：值得继续查的线索，不能用于采购确认。
- `CONTRADICTED`：关键主张被更强证据推翻。
- `UNRESOLVED`：证据不足或相互冲突。
- `HOLD`：因过期、暂停、变更或新反证等待复核。

## 多轴结论，不输出厂家概率

固定分开写 `trace_stage`、分析置信度、ESS、能力适配、认证状态、验证优先级和采购效用。禁止把任何一轴改写成“厂家概率”，也不提供未校准的人工百分比。用户要求百分比时，说明当前没有独立真值集和外部校准，只能提供上述可审计多轴判断。

候选可因图片、产品族和贸易关系获得较高相对排序，但只要制造地点或目标工序未闭合，就不得写成“真正厂家”。

## 事实、推断和建议

每段分别标记：

- 事实：来源直接支持的内容。
- 推断：由哪些事实推得、有哪些替代解释。
- 建议：下一步询证、样品或现场动作。

不要在事实句中混入“因此一定是”“显然”“唯一可能”等结论词。

## 联系方式

给出联系人、电话、邮箱、微信、WhatsApp、官网、LinkedIn 和证据 URL。标记官方公布、平台公开、历史材料或用户验证；没有公开号码时不要猜测。

同时记录最后核验时间，不收集无关私人信息。

## 结论摘要

摘要必须同时列出：

- 当前领先候选及其明确支持范围。
- 未闭合门槛。
- 最强反证或替代解释。
- 认证当前状态和查询日期。
- 下一步最有信息增益的动作。

## 发布前检查

确认所有链接可点击、文件路径真实、证据哈希匹配、候选排序与正文一致、过期证书未标活跃、浏览器阻断未标已核验、报告没有厂家百分比、`operational PASS` 未冒充厂家确认。

公开发布须排除私密联系、客户/供应商记录、凭据、会话数据和未脱敏证据；真实案件另存受保护档案。
