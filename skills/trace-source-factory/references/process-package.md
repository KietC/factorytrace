# Process Evidence and On-Site Production Capability

## English

### Objective and evidence package

Prove that the target process actually occurs at the specified site, not merely an adjacent capability. Request:

- Legal entity, manufacturing site, and business site contact.
- Exact model/drawing/BOM revision and material specification.
- Process flow with in-house/outsourced boundaries.
- Raw-material batch/heat, incoming certificates, and supplier.
- Equipment names/models/tonnage/serials/count/workshop location.
- Tool/fixture ID, ownership, custody site, revision, and trials.
- Production order, batch, date, shift, and yield.
- Inspection methods, equipment calibration, raw tests, and nonconformance handling.
- Labels, CoC, packing list, CoO, and dispatch records.

### Continuous-video request

Use one continuous sequence: gate/address → workshop → material → equipment plate → tooling ID → loading → target process → semi-finished part → measurement → finished part/batch label. An agreed fresh challenge phrase/order/random action helps detect reused video. Do not request public disclosure of secret drawings; redacted drawings, limited dimensions, tooling IDs, or issuer confirmation may suffice.

### Process-specific interpretation

- Forging: heating, blanking, pressing, trimming, cleaning, and subsequent machining.
- Deep drawing: sheet/blanking, drawing tool, suitable press, trimming, and finishing.
- Die casting: melt, casting machine/tool, gate removal, and finishing.
- Toolmaking: design/material, CNC, wire cutting/EDM, heat treatment, assembly, and trials.
- Assembly: proves assembly only, not in-house component/tool manufacture.
- Testing: identify instrument, calibration, method, limits, and raw result.

### Outsourcing and inquiry order

For each outsourced step, bind supplier entity/site, order, two-way logistics, batches, and quality responsibility. “Partner factory” without verifiable bindings leaves the upstream factory unidentified.

Start with less sensitive questions: in-house or outsourced, equipment type, city, and process boundaries. Then request address/plates/tool IDs/batch documents/video. Record refusals, vagueness, and inconsistencies as limitations or contradictions rather than filling gaps. Contacting a supplier requires the user's authority; this template does not grant it.

## 中文

# 工艺包与现场生产能力

## 目标

证明目标工序在指定地点实际发生，而不是只证明企业具备相邻能力。

## 工艺包字段

要求候选提供：

- 法律主体、制造地点和现场联系人。
- 精确产品型号、图号、BOM 版本和材料规范。
- 工艺流程图及厂内/外协边界。
- 原料批次、炉号、来料证书和供应商。
- 设备名称、型号、吨位、序列号、数量和所在车间。
- 模具/工装编号、所有权、保管地点、版本和试模记录。
- 生产订单、批次、日期、班次和良率。
- 检验规范、设备校准、原始测试结果和不合格处置。
- 包装标签、CoC、装箱单、CoO 和发货记录。

## 连续视频脚本

要求一次连续拍摄：厂门/地址标识 → 车间 → 原料 → 设备铭牌 → 模具编号 → 上料 → 目标工序 → 半成品 → 测量 → 成品/批次标签。使用当天挑战词、订单号或随机动作降低旧视频复用风险。

不要要求公开商业机密图纸；可用遮盖图、局部尺寸、模具编号和认证机构确认完成最小披露。

## 工序判定

- 热锻：看加热、下料、锻压、切边、清理和后续机加工的连续性。
- 深拉：看板材、开料、拉伸模、大吨位液压/机械压力机、修边和表面处理。
- 压铸：看熔料、压铸机、模具、去浇口和后处理。
- 模具制造：看设计、材料、CNC、线切割/EDM、热处理、装配和试模。
- 装配：只能支持装配，不能反推阀体、盘体或模具在厂内制造。
- 测试：必须包含量具/仪器身份、校准状态、方法、阈值和原始结果。

## 外协穿透

对每个外协步骤记录供应商法律主体、地点、订单、来回物流、批次映射和质量责任。若候选只愿确认“有合作工厂”而拒绝可验证绑定，保持上游工厂未识别。

## 询证策略

从低敏感问题开始：是否厂内完成、设备类型、城市和工序边界。再请求地址、设备铭牌、模具编号、批次单据和现场视频。把拒绝、模糊回答和前后矛盾记录为限制或反证，不自行补全。

向供应商发送询证须有用户授权，模板本身不授予外部联系权限。
